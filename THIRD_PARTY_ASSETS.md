# Third-party assets

Everything in this repository that was not generated by the project itself is
listed here with its source and the terms it was obtained under.

## Recorded weapon and impact audio — `assets/audio/weapons/`

Taken from the **Sonniss.com GDC Game Audio Bundles**, which Sonniss distributes
free of charge for use in games and other media:

| Files | Original library | Author |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ak_01..05.ogg` | Battle Rifles & Pistols (`BR AK47 Burst With Tail`) | Watson Wu |
| `m4_01..05.ogg` | Enfield L85 Rifle 5.56 mm | Pole Position Production |
| `pkm_01..06.ogg` | MG 42 machine gun | Pole Position |
| `svd_01..05.ogg` | Mosin-Nagant M38 bolt-action rifle | Pole Position |
| `50cal_01..04.ogg`, `dushka_01..03.ogg` | derived from the MG 42 takes by offline pitch shift | Pole Position |
| `rpg_launch.ogg` | Detonation — Explosion (`weapon_smoke_grenade_launcher`) | BluezoneCorp |
| `impact_dirt.ogg`, `impact_glass.ogg`, `impact_flesh.ogg` | Bullet Bys & Impacts | PMSFX |
| `mortar.ogg` | Detonation — Explosion (`explosion_mortar`) | BluezoneCorp |
| `big_explosion.ogg`, `far_explosion.ogg` | Explosions | Stefano Cremona |

The files here are **derivatives**: single shots were cut out of the original
takes, normalised, and given an outdoor tail, then encoded as mono Ogg Vorbis at
44.1 kHz. They are part of this game's sound design and are not redistributed as
a standalone sound library. Sonniss' bundle licence permits use in commercial
products but forbids reselling or redistributing the sounds on their own; keep
that in mind before publishing the raw sample folder elsewhere.

## Photographed material textures — `assets/runtime/materials/`

Brick, painted plaster, weathered planks, corrugated steel and concrete surface
sets from **[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)**, released under **CC0 1.0**
(public domain): `Bricks060`, `PaintedPlaster017`, `Planks037A`,
`CorrugatedSteel009`, `Concrete046`. Only the 1K colour, OpenGL normal and
roughness maps are included.

## Terrain — `assets/runtime/terrain/`

Rocky gravel albedo, normal and roughness maps from ambientCG, **CC0 1.0**.

## AK-74M model and its recordings — `assets/runtime/models`, `assets/runtime/audio`

Imported with the project's original asset drop; see `assets/runtime/weapon_manifest.json`
for the file list.
